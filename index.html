Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

When is tax audit applicable?

Tax audit under Section 44AB is applicable when business turnover exceeds ₹1 crore (₹10 crore if cash transactions are within 5%). For professionals, the limit is ₹50 lakhs. It's also applicable when you opt out of the presumptive taxation scheme.

How frequently do I need to file GST returns?

GST returns are typically filed monthly (GSTR-3B and GSTR-1) for businesses with turnover above ₹5 crore. Businesses below this threshold can opt for quarterly filing under the QRMP scheme. Annual return (GSTR-9) is due by December 31.

What are the basic compliance requirements for startups?

Startups need to handle company registration (MCA filings), GST registration if applicable, TDS compliance, income tax filing, ROC annual returns, and statutory audits. It's crucial to maintain proper books of accounts from day one.

What is the difference between CA and tax consultant?

A Chartered Accountant (CA) is a certified professional regulated by ICAI who can perform statutory audits, certify financial statements, and represent clients before tax authorities. A tax consultant may or may not be a CA. Only CAs can sign audit reports.

How can I reduce my income tax legally?

You can reduce tax through Section 80C investments (PPF, ELSS, Life Insurance), NPS contributions (80CCD), health insurance premiums (80D), home loan interest (Section 24), HRA claims, and choosing between old and new tax regimes based on your situation.

Do you handle GST registration for new businesses?

Yes, we handle end-to-end GST registration including application preparation, document compilation, filing, and follow-up with the GST department. We also advise on whether GST registration is mandatory or voluntary for your business type.

What documents do I need for income tax filing?

You'll need Form 16 (for salaried), bank statements, investment proofs (PPF, insurance, ELSS), home loan certificate, rent receipts, capital gains statements, and any other income documentation. We guide you through the complete list based on your profile.
